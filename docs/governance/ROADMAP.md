## Risk Management

### Technical Risks

- **Blockchain volatility** → Mitigate with multi-chain support
- **AI hallucinations** → Implement human oversight checkpoints
- **Compliance changes** → Quarterly policy review process

### Operational Risks

- **Resource constraints** → Phased rollout, community contributions
- **Scope creep** → Strict phase gates, MVP-first approach
- **Burnout** → Automated workflows, AI-assisted operations

### Legal Risks

- **Regulatory changes** → Stay informed, adaptive policy framework
- **Liability** → Insurance, clear disclaimers, limited scope
- **Data breaches** → Zero-knowledge architecture, encryption everywhere
